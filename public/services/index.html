Electrician

Repairs, upgrades and the big jobs that keep a house safe and working. We troubleshoot problems, replace outdated panels, fuse boxes and breakers, rewire older homes and fix inspection items for homeowners and small businesses around Yardley.

Electrician overview

  • Electrical Panel Upgrade

    Replace an outdated or overloaded electrical panel with a modern 200-amp service. We handle the permit, the PECO disconnect and reconnect, the inspection, and label every circuit when we're done.

    Learn more →

  • Electrical Subpanel Installation

    Add circuits where you need them with a subpanel for a garage, basement, addition or workshop. We check your service capacity first, size the feeder properly and handle the permit and inspection.

    Learn more →

  • Fuse Box Replacement

    Replace an old fuse box with a modern circuit breaker panel, or get a blown fuse changed safely. We make sure every fuse is the right size for its wire and upgrade the service when it's due.

    Learn more →

  • Home Rewiring

    Replace knob-and-tube, cloth-insulated or ungrounded wiring in older homes, room by room or all at once. We fish new cable through existing walls wherever we can, so you get new wiring with as little patching as possible.

    Learn more →

  • Aluminum Wiring Replacement

    Homes wired with single-strand aluminum in the 1960s and 70s can overheat at outlets and switches. We make every connection safe with approved copper pigtail connectors, or replace the aluminum wiring entirely.

    Learn more →

  • Whole House Surge Protection

    A surge protector installed at your electrical panel protects everything in the house, from the furnace and fridge to TVs and computers, against surges from lightning, the utility and your own large appliances.

    Learn more →

  • Electrical Repair & Troubleshooting

    Dead outlets, tripping breakers, flickering lights, buzzing switches and half-working circuits, tracked down methodically and fixed properly. We find the cause instead of guessing, and explain what we found.

    Learn more →

  • Electrical Inspections & Maintenance

    A thorough check of your home's electrical system: panel inspection, tightening connections, testing GFCI and AFCI protection, smoke and CO alarms, and a written list of anything that needs attention.

    Learn more →
