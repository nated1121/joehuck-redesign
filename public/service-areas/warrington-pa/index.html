Homes in Warrington

Warrington grew rapidly in the 1980s, 1990s and 2000s, so much of its housing is colonials, townhouses and newer single-family homes. These homes generally have 200 amp services and sound wiring. What owners want is more from them: finished basements, EV chargers, better lighting than the builder installed, outdoor living spaces and smart controls. Older homes along the main roads and in the township's earlier neighborhoods have the same needs as other mid-century homes, including panel upgrades and more circuits.

Electrical Work We Do in Warrington

Our most common Warrington jobs are EV charger installation, basement wiring, recessed lighting and smart lighting. We also install outdoor outlets and landscape lighting, add whole-house surge protection and upgrade panels in older homes.

Upgrading a Builder-Grade Home

Many Warrington homes came with a single light fixture per room, few outdoor outlets and no provisions for EVs or finished basements. We help homeowners plan upgrades that make these homes work better: recessed and under-cabinet lighting, dimmers and smart switches, outlets where they're actually needed, and circuits for the garage and basement.

Around Warrington

We work throughout the township, from neighborhoods along Easton Road and Street Road to the areas around the Valley Square shopping center and the Central Bucks schools. Warrington is a longer drive from our base in Yardley than our lower Bucks towns, so we group appointments to keep scheduling reliable.

Common Electrical Issues in Warrington Homes

In newer homes, the most common needs are capacity planning for EVs and basements, builder-grade lighting and missing outdoor outlets. In older homes, it's full panels and too few circuits. We'll explain your options clearly.

Outdoor Living

Patios, decks, outdoor kitchens and hot tubs are popular upgrades in Warrington's newer neighborhoods. We install weatherproof outlets, landscape and deck lighting, circuits for outdoor kitchens and heaters, and hot tub wiring with the required GFCI disconnect. Everything outdoors is GFCI-protected and rated for wet locations, and we plan it with your deck or patio builder.

My house has a 200 amp panel. Can I add an EV charger?

In most cases, yes. We run a load calculation first to confirm there's room.

Can you wire my basement before the drywall goes up?

Yes. We rough in the wiring, pass inspection and return to install devices and fixtures after finishing.

Can you replace builder-grade light fixtures throughout my house?

Yes. We can replace all of them in one visit, check every box and connection, and haul away the old fixtures.
