Homes in Morrisville

Much of Morrisville Borough was built in the late 1800s and early 1900s, with more homes added through the mid-century. That means a lot of older wiring: knob-and-tube, cloth-covered cable, fuse boxes, ungrounded outlets and panels that have been added onto more than once. In twins and rowhomes, wiring routes can be tight, and we plan carefully to avoid unnecessary damage.

Many Morrisville properties are rentals, and landlords need electrical items fixed quickly to pass inspections and keep units rented.

Electrical Work We Do in Morrisville

Our most common Morrisville jobs are home rewiring, fuse box replacement, ground wire installation for ungrounded outlets, and electrical repair and troubleshooting. We also handle code corrections for rental inspections and sales, and install smoke and CO detectors.

Common Electrical Issues in Morrisville Homes

The issues we find most often in Morrisville are knob-and-tube and cloth-insulated wiring, fuse boxes, ungrounded outlets, overloaded circuits and amateur wiring from past repairs. In twins and rowhomes, shared walls and tight spaces make careful planning important. We'll explain what we find in plain language and give you options, with safety items first.
