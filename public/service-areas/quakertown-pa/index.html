Homes in Quakertown

Quakertown's borough neighborhoods have many homes from the late 1800s and early 1900s, including Victorians, twins and row homes near Broad Street and the downtown. The surrounding townships are mostly homes built from the 1970s onward. Older borough homes often need rewiring, fuse box replacement and grounding; newer homes need more capacity for EVs, finished basements and outdoor projects.

Service Upgrades With the Borough Utility

Quakertown Borough operates its own electric utility, so service work in the borough is coordinated with Quakertown's electric department rather than PECO, and the utility has its own requirements for meters and service equipment. We handle that coordination on every panel upgrade. Homes in the surrounding townships may be served by a different utility; we'll check yours when we look at the job.

Electrical Work We Do in Quakertown

Our most common Quakertown jobs are panel upgrades, home rewiring, electrical code corrections for sales and rentals, and EV charger installation in the township neighborhoods. We also install lighting, add GFCI protection and handle repairs.

Around Quakertown

We work throughout the borough, from the downtown near Liberty Hall, where the Liberty Bell is said to have stopped on its way to Allentown in 1777, to the neighborhoods near Memorial Park and the Quakertown Farmers Market on Route 309. Quakertown is one of the farther towns we serve from our base in Yardley, so we schedule upper Bucks appointments together.

Common Electrical Issues in Quakertown Homes

In older borough homes, it's knob-and-tube and cloth-insulated wiring, fuse boxes and ungrounded outlets. In township homes, it's panels without room for EVs, hot tubs and finished basements. We'll explain what we find in plain language and give you options.

Businesses Downtown

Quakertown's downtown and the Route 309 corridor are home to many small businesses in buildings of every age. We install LED lighting, circuits for equipment and signage power, and handle repairs, scheduling work around your hours. In older buildings, we'll also look at whether the service and panel can support what you're adding, since many were sized for much smaller loads.
