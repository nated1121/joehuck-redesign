Homes in Bensalem

Much of Bensalem was built between the 1950s and the 1970s. The older homes often still have small services and panels with no room left, while some homes and townhouses built in the late 1960s and early 1970s have aluminum branch wiring that needs proper aluminum wiring repair at every connection. Newer townhouses and condos are generally wired well, but owners want better lighting, EV chargers and more capacity.

Electrical Work We Do in Bensalem

Our most common Bensalem jobs are panel upgrades, aluminum wiring remediation, circuit breaker replacement in aging panels, and recessed lighting in kitchens and living rooms. We also handle code corrections when homes are sold and rental inspection items for landlords.

Small Businesses in Bensalem

Bensalem's commercial corridors along Street Road, Route 1 and Bristol Pike are full of offices, shops, restaurants and light industrial spaces. We help businesses with LED lighting retrofits, new circuits for equipment, exterior and parking lot lighting, and repairs, working after hours when that keeps you open.

Around Bensalem

We work all over the township, from neighborhoods near Neshaminy State Park and the Andalusia estate along the river to the areas around Street Road and the Neshaminy Mall. We're a short drive up I-95 from our base in Yardley, so scheduling is easy.

Common Electrical Issues in Bensalem Homes

In older homes, it's full panels, ungrounded outlets and too few kitchen circuits. In homes from the aluminum wiring era, it's warm outlets and switches and connections that need attention. In townhouses, it's often panels that were sized tight from the start. We'll explain what we find and give you clear options.

Townhouse and Condo Communities

Bensalem has many townhouse and condo communities from the 1970s onward. Panels in these units were often sized tightly, and some of the older ones have aluminum wiring. We work inside individual units, and when a job affects exterior walls, meters or shared spaces, we coordinate with the association or property manager before we start. Landlords with several units can have us look at them together and plan upgrades in phases.
