Homes in Perkasie

Perkasie's older neighborhoods are full of homes from the late 1800s and early 1900s, when the town grew around the railroad and its factories, with mid-century and newer homes built around them. The older homes often have wiring from several eras, fuse boxes or small panels, and ungrounded outlets. The newer ones need more capacity and better lighting for today's households.

Service Upgrades With the Borough Utility

Perkasie runs its own municipal electric utility, so service upgrades here are coordinated with the borough rather than PECO. The utility sets its own requirements for meters, service entrances and scheduling disconnects and reconnects. We coordinate with them on every panel upgrade and service change, so you don't have to.

Electrical Work We Do in Perkasie

Besides panel upgrades, our most common Perkasie jobs are home rewiring for older homes, fuse box replacement and electrical repair and troubleshooting. We also install lighting and ceiling fans, add GFCI protection and handle code corrections for sales in the Pennridge school district.

Around Perkasie

We work throughout the borough, from the neighborhoods around Market Street and the downtown to the areas near Lenape Park, with its carousel, and the South Perkasie Covered Bridge. Perkasie is one of the farther towns we serve from our base in Yardley, so we schedule upper Bucks appointments together to keep them reliable.

Common Electrical Issues in Perkasie Homes

The issues we see most are older wiring, fuse boxes, ungrounded outlets and panels without room for new circuits. We'll explain what we find in plain language and give you options, safety items first.

Small Businesses Downtown

Perkasie's downtown has shops, restaurants and offices, many in older buildings. We help businesses with lighting, new circuits for equipment and repairs, and we schedule the work around business hours. For older commercial buildings, we'll also look at the service and panel, since many were sized for much smaller loads than today's equipment and lighting draw.
