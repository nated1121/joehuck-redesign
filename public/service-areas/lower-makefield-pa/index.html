Homes in Lower Makefield and What They Need

Lower Makefield grew in waves. There are early post-war capes and ranchers near Yardley, 1960s and 1970s colonials and splits across the middle of the township, and larger homes from the 1980s through the 2000s farther out. Each era brings its own electrical issues.

Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have 100 amp services and too few kitchen circuits. Some houses built in the late 1960s and early 1970s have aluminum branch wiring, which needs proper aluminum wiring repair at every connection. Newer homes are usually wired well, but their owners want more from them: EV chargers, finished basements, and better lighting than the builder's single fixture per room.

Electrical Work We Do in Lower Makefield

Our most common jobs in the township are panel upgrades for homes adding major loads, EV charger installation in attached garages, and recessed lighting in kitchens and family rooms. We also finish basements with new circuits and lighting, add outdoor outlets for decks and patios, and handle electrical code corrections when homes go on the market. Lower Makefield's housing market moves quickly, and we can usually turn inspection items around before closing.

We work on small businesses in the township too, from office lighting retrofits to new circuits for equipment.
