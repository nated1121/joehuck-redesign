Homes in Richboro and Northampton Township

Most of Northampton Township was built from the 1960s through the 1990s: colonials, split-levels and ranchers on generous lots, with some older farmhouses and newer custom homes mixed in. The homes are generally well built, but many of their electrical systems are original. Panels are often full, kitchens and basements need more circuits, and families are adding pools, hot tubs, EV chargers and home offices that the original service was never sized for.

Electrical Work We Do in Richboro

Our most common Richboro jobs are panel upgrades, EV charger installation, basement wiring and hot tub electrical hookups. We also install landscape and outdoor lighting, add whole-house surge protection and handle code corrections for homes being sold in the Council Rock school district.

Outdoor Projects

Larger lots in Northampton Township mean a lot of outdoor work: pool and hot tub circuits, landscape lighting, power to sheds and detached garages, and floodlights for long driveways. We plan each run for the distance and load, trench in conduit where needed, and handle the permit and inspection.

Around Richboro

We work throughout Northampton Township, from Richboro's center along Second Street Pike to Holland, Churchville and the neighborhoods near Tyler State Park and the Churchville Nature Center. Being based nearby in Yardley means we can schedule quickly and come back easily when needed.

Common Electrical Issues in Richboro Homes

The issues we see most are full panels in homes adding major loads, too few circuits in kitchens and basements, and outdoor wiring from past projects that doesn't meet current code. We'll explain what we find in plain language and give you options.

Older Farmhouses in the Township

Scattered through Northampton Township are older farmhouses that predate the surrounding neighborhoods. These homes often have small services, wiring from several eras and outbuildings fed by old underground or overhead cable. We inspect what's there, recommend what needs to be replaced, and plan the work so the house stays livable while we do it.
