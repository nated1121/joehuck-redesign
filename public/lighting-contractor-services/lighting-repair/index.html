Lighting Problems We Fix

  • Lights that flicker or pulse, on a dimmer or not
  • Fixtures that buzz or hum
  • Lights that don't turn on, even with a new bulb
  • Fluorescent fixtures that flicker, hum or won't light
  • Bulbs in high ceilings, foyers and stairwells you can't reach
  • Outdoor, porch and landscape lights that have quit

Finding the Cause

Flicker has many causes: an incompatible dimmer, a loose connection at the fixture or switch, a failing LED driver or a worn socket. Flicker across several rooms can point to a loose connection at the panel or service, which needs attention soon; see electrical repair and troubleshooting. We test the circuit, the switch and the fixture to find the cause, then fix it.

Ballast and Bulb Replacement

Fluorescent fixtures use a ballast that eventually fails, causing flicker, humming or dark tubes. We can replace the ballast, but in many cases converting the fixture to LED makes more sense; see LED lighting retrofits. We also replace bulbs in fixtures that are hard or unsafe to reach, like two-story foyers, cathedral ceilings and stairwells, and can switch them to long-life LEDs so you won't need us again for years.

Outdoor and Landscape Lights

Outdoor fixtures face weather, and moisture causes corrosion and failures. We repair and replace porch lights, floodlights, post lights and landscape lighting, and fix tripped or failed transformers and photocells.

What Affects the Cost

Most lighting repairs are quick once the cause is found. High ceilings, several fixtures and replacement parts affect the price, which we'll give you before doing the work.

When to Replace Instead of Repair

If a fixture has corroded sockets, brittle wiring from years of heat, or cracked housings, replacing it is often cheaper than repairing it. Older fixtures in closets and attics may lack the protection that modern fixtures have. We'll tell you when a repair makes sense and when a new fixture is the better value, and give you prices for both.

LED Retrofits

If your fixtures are old fluorescents, halogens or recessed cans, an LED retrofit often costs less over time than repairing them. We bypass ballasts, install LED retrofit kits or replace fixtures outright, in homes and small businesses.

Questions

Good to Know

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Why do my lights flicker?

Usually a loose bulb, an incompatible dimmer, a loose connection or a failing driver. Flicker in several rooms can mean a problem at the panel.

Should I replace my fluorescent ballast or switch to LED?

If the fixture is in good shape, either works. LED conversion usually costs about the same and ends ballast failures for good.

Can you change bulbs in my two-story foyer?

Yes. We bring the equipment to work safely at height and can switch you to long-life LEDs.

Why does my light bulb keep burning out?

Common causes are a loose socket, too much heat in an enclosed fixture, voltage problems or the wrong bulb type. We check the fixture and circuit to find it.
