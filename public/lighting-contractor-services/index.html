Overview

Many projects combine a few types of light: recessed lights for general light, pendants or a chandelier as the focal point, and under-cabinet or accent lighting for tasks and atmosphere. We'll help you plan the layout, the switching and the dimming so it all works together.

If your lights flicker, buzz or won't turn on, start with lighting repair.

Smart Lighting

Smart switches and dimmers let you set scenes and schedules and control lights by app or voice. Most need a neutral wire, which older homes often don't have at the switch, so we add it and pair each switch with compatible bulbs and fixtures so the system works reliably.
