When to Call an Electrician

  • The new fixture is heavier than the one it replaces
  • A room has only a switched outlet and no ceiling light
  • You want a light where there's no box, like over an island or beside a mirror
  • The existing box is loose, or the wires are brittle or scorched
  • The fixture goes on a high or sloped ceiling

Ceiling Lights

We remove the old fixture, check the box and wiring, and fix what isn't right: a loose or undersized box, wire insulation made brittle by years of heat, or a missing ground. Many older bedrooms and living rooms have a switched outlet instead of a ceiling light, so we run a new switch leg through the attic or from below and install a properly supported box. Replacing every builder-grade fixture in one visit is efficient and gives the house a consistent look.

Chandeliers

A chandelier needs a box and support rated for its weight, and it has to hang at the right height: about 30 to 36 inches above a dining table, and high enough to walk under in a foyer. We assemble it, hang it level and put it on a dimmer. For two-story foyers, we bring the ladders and staging to do it safely.

Pendant Lights

Pendants over a kitchen island or peninsula are spaced evenly along its length and hung so they light the counter without blocking the view across the room. We add boxes where they're needed, use adapters for sloped ceilings and put the pendants on their own switch or dimmer.

Wall Sconces

Sconces beside a bathroom mirror light your face evenly, without the shadows an overhead light casts. We also install them along hallways and stairs, beside the bed and at exterior doors, fishing new boxes into finished walls at the right height.

Track Lighting

Modern LED track and monorail lighting lets you aim light at artwork, a kitchen work area or a display, and move the heads later. It works well on sloped ceilings, where recessed lights can be hard to fit.

What Affects the Cost

Straight swaps are quick, especially several in one visit. New boxes and switch legs, high ceilings, heavy fixtures and chandeliers that need a lot of assembly take longer. Your written estimate covers all of it before any work starts.

Good to Know

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Can I replace a light fixture myself?

Many people do simple swaps. If the box is loose, the wires are brittle or the fixture is heavy, it's worth having an electrician do it.

How high should a chandelier hang over a dining table?

Usually 30 to 36 inches above the tabletop, a little higher with taller ceilings. We'll set it where it looks and works best.

Do you supply the fixture?

We can, or we'll install the one you've chosen.
