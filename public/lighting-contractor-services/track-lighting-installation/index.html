When Track Lighting Makes Sense

  • You want to light artwork, shelves or a gallery wall
  • The ceiling is sloped, beamed or too shallow for recessed lights
  • A kitchen or studio needs light aimed at work areas
  • A shop or gallery changes its displays often
  • You have old halogen track that runs hot and burns through bulbs

Track, Monorail and Cable

Standard track holds heads you can slide and aim anywhere along its length. Monorail systems can be bent to follow curves and mounted on standoffs, which works well on sloped or high ceilings. We'll help you choose a system and heads for how you'll use the space, with LED heads in the right beam spread and color temperature.

Installation

Track can often be fed from an existing ceiling box, so there's no need for new wiring in the ceiling. We mount the track securely, level it, set the heads and aim them. Low-voltage systems need a transformer located where it can be reached. Everything goes on a dimmer compatible with the system.

Updating Old Track

Old halogen track heads are hot and inefficient. We can replace the heads with LED versions on the same track, if it's in good shape, or replace the whole system. For offices and shops, see LED lighting retrofits.

Track or Something Else?

For flush general lighting, recessed lights may suit you better. For highlighting shelves, coves and artwork with a softer look, see accent and cove lighting.

What Affects the Cost

The track length, the number of heads, whether a new feed location is needed and the system type drive the price.

Designing for Artwork

For artwork, aim heads so light hits the art at about a 30-degree angle, which reduces glare on glass and canvas. Narrow beams highlight small pieces; wider beams cover larger works. High color-rendering LEDs make colors look accurate. We'll set the heads and adjust them with you so each piece looks its best.

Questions

Good to Know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Can track lighting go on a sloped ceiling?

Yes. Monorail and many track systems mount on sloped and vaulted ceilings.

Can I reuse my old track with LED heads?

Often, if the track is in good shape and the new heads are compatible with it.

Is track lighting good for art?

It's one of the best options, because you can aim and reposition heads as your art changes.

Is LED track lighting dimmable?

Most LED track heads are dimmable with a compatible dimmer. Low-voltage systems need a dimmer that works with their transformer, which we match when we install the system.
