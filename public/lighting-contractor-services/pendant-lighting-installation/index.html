Common Pendant Projects

  • Two or three pendants over a kitchen island
  • Replacing a single island fixture with pendants
  • Pendants over a peninsula, bar or breakfast table
  • Pendants on sloped or vaulted ceilings
  • A pendant in an entry or over a bathroom vanity

Spacing and Height

Pendants over an island usually hang about 30 to 36 inches above the countertop, high enough to see across the room and low enough to light the work surface. Spacing depends on the island's length and the pendants' size; as a rule, they should be evenly spaced, with some clearance from each end. We measure before cutting and mark the positions with you.

New Boxes and Wiring

When you're going from one fixture to two or three pendants, each one needs its own box, and we run wiring between them in the ceiling, usually through the floor above or the attic, with small openings. On sloped ceilings we use adapters so pendants hang straight. Multi-light canopies can hang several pendants from a single box when that's the better option.

Switching and Dimming

Pendants usually go on their own switch and dimmer, separate from the room's recessed lights, so you can set the mood. They pair naturally with under-cabinet lighting and are often part of a larger kitchen electrical upgrade. For a larger single fixture, see chandelier installation.

What Affects the Cost

Replacing an existing fixture with one pendant is quick. Adding new boxes for multiple pendants, sloped ceilings and finished ceilings above take more time.

Choosing Pendants

Pendant size should suit the island and the room. Larger pendants make a statement but can block sight lines, while small pendants work well in groups of three. Clear glass pendants show the bulb, so the bulb choice matters; frosted and opaque shades hide it. Adjustable-height cords or chains make it easier to fine-tune the hanging height after installation. We'll help you check that the pendants you like will work with your ceiling height, your island and the dimmer we install. If you're still choosing, bring us the options and we'll tell you what each needs.

Kitchens Without Island Wiring

If your island has no ceiling box above it, we'll add one, running the wiring through the ceiling from a nearby switch location.
