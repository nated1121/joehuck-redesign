When to Add Security Lighting

  • Dark driveways, side yards or back doors
  • Old halogen floods that run hot and burn out
  • Cameras that can't see at night
  • A detached garage, barn or shed with no outdoor light
  • A business lot or entrance that needs to be lit after hours

Choosing the Right Lights

LED floodlights use far less power than old halogen fixtures and last much longer. We choose brightness and beam spread for the area, and color temperature that looks good and helps cameras. Many homes pair a floodlight at the driveway with motion sensor lighting at the doors.

Controls

Security lights can run dusk-to-dawn with a photocell, turn on with motion, or both: dim all night and full brightness on motion. They can also be scheduled or controlled from your phone with smart switches. We set the controls to match how you use your property.

Aiming and Mounting

We mount fixtures high enough to cover the area and aim them down, which lights your property effectively and keeps glare out of the neighbors' windows and drivers' eyes. Fixtures on older homes, detached buildings and barns may need new wiring and boxes. Runs to detached buildings go underground in conduit.

Beyond Security

For lighting that makes the yard look good, see landscape lighting. For outlets for holiday lights and tools, see outdoor outlet installation.

What Affects the Cost

Replacing existing floods is quick. New locations, high mounting, brick or stone walls and runs to detached buildings take more time.

Lighting and Cameras

Cameras record much better with good lighting. Floodlights that come on with motion give cameras clear, color footage instead of grainy night vision. Some combined camera-floodlight units need a strong mounting point and steady power; we'll install the box and wiring they need. We can also add outlets or power for standalone cameras near the lights, so everything is powered and nothing depends on batteries.

Lighting for Businesses

For commercial lots, walkways and entrances, we install LED wall packs and area lights, set on photocells or timers, and repair or replace failing fixtures.

Questions

Good to Know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Can floodlights be on a motion sensor and a timer?

Yes. Many fixtures run dim all night and go to full brightness on motion.

How do I keep floodlights from bothering neighbors?

Mount them high and aim them down at your property. We choose fixtures with good glare control.

Are LED floodlights bright enough?

Yes. They're brighter than halogen per watt and last years longer.

Can you replace old halogen floodlights?

Yes. We replace them with LED fixtures that use far less power, run cooler and last much longer, often on the same box.
