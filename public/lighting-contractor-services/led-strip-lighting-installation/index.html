  • Behind TVs, mirrors and headboards

Doing Strip Lighting Right

The difference between strip lighting that looks professional and strip lighting that looks cheap comes down to details. We mount strips in aluminum channels with diffusers, which hide the individual dots, protect the strip and pull heat away so the LEDs last. We choose the strip density and brightness for the location, and match color temperature to the room's other lights.

Drivers and Voltage Drop

LED strips run on low voltage, usually 12 or 24 volts, from a driver. Long runs lose voltage along the way, which makes the far end dimmer or a different color. We size drivers for the total load, feed long runs from more than one point, and keep drivers in accessible, ventilated locations. Hardwired drivers get a switch or dimmer, so the lights work like any other light in the house.

Controls

Strip lighting can be dimmed, and tunable-white or color strips can change with a remote, app or smart system. We'll help you choose controls that are simple to use; see smart lighting installation. For coves and tray ceilings, see accent and cove lighting. For kitchen counters, see under-cabinet lighting.

What Affects the Cost

Length of the runs, channels and diffusers, the number of drivers and zones, and how the wiring is concealed drive the price.

Waterproof Strips and Outdoor Use

Strip lighting in bathrooms, near sinks, outdoors or in damp basements needs the right protection rating, typically a silicone-coated or fully sealed strip, and a driver located where it will stay dry. We'll choose strips rated for the location. For outdoor steps and railings, we use channels and strips designed for exposure to weather.

Planning for Service

LED strips last a long time, but drivers can fail. We keep drivers in accessible spots, never sealed behind drywall or cabinetry, so replacing one later is simple.

Why do LED strips need a channel?

A channel with a diffuser hides the dots of light, protects the strip and helps it stay cool, so it lasts longer.

Can LED strips be dimmed?

Yes, with a dimmable driver and a compatible dimmer or controller.

Why is the end of my strip dimmer than the start?

Voltage drop on a long run. Feeding the strip from more than one point fixes it.
