Good Places for Motion Lighting

  • Closets, pantries and laundry rooms
  • Garages and basements, where you walk in with full hands
  • Stairways and hallways at night
  • Driveways, walkways and side yards
  • Front, back and garage doors

Indoor Sensors: Occupancy vs. Vacancy

Occupancy sensors turn lights on automatically when you enter and off after you leave. Vacancy sensors require you to turn lights on, then turn them off automatically; they're a good fit for bedrooms and offices, where automatic on isn't always wanted. We replace ordinary switches with sensor switches, choose the right coverage for the room, and adjust the timeout and sensitivity so lights don't shut off while you're still there.

Outdoor Motion Lights

Outdoor motion lights light your way and discourage intruders. We install LED fixtures with built-in sensors, or add sensors to existing fixtures, aimed at the areas you care about rather than the street. Many combine a dusk-to-dawn low light with full brightness on motion. For stronger lighting on driveways and yards, see security and flood lighting.

Tuning It So It Works

Poorly aimed sensors either miss you or trigger on passing cars and swaying branches. We set the angle, range, sensitivity and time settings at the job, and come back to adjust them if needed. Motion sensors also pair well with smart lighting systems. In garages, see garage lighting installation for brighter fixtures.

What Affects the Cost

Swapping a switch for a sensor is quick. Adding new outdoor fixtures, especially where there's no existing wiring, takes more time.

Motion Sensors for Safety

Motion lighting on stairs and hallways helps prevent falls at night, especially for older family members. Low-level lighting that comes on with motion, like a lit toe-kick, a step light or a sensor-controlled hallway light, lets people see where they're going without turning on bright overhead lights. We can add these in finished homes with minimal disruption.

Outdoor Sensors and Cameras

If you have security cameras, motion lights near them help cameras capture clearer images at night. We'll position the lights so they don't glare into the camera lens.

What's the difference between occupancy and vacancy sensors?

Occupancy sensors turn lights on and off automatically. Vacancy sensors only turn them off; you turn them on.

Why does my motion light keep turning on by itself?

It's likely picking up cars, trees or animals. Adjusting the angle and sensitivity usually fixes it.

Can you add a motion sensor to my existing outdoor light?

Often, yes, with an add-on sensor or by replacing the fixture.

Can a motion sensor work with LED lights?

Yes. Most modern sensor switches work with LED fixtures. We choose models rated for LED loads so the lights don't flicker or stay dimly lit.
