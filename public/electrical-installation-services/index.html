Questions

Good to Know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Can you add outlets without opening my walls?

Usually. We fish new cable from the basement or attic and cut only a small opening for the new box.

Do small installations need a permit?

Replacing a device like-for-like usually doesn't. New circuits and most new wiring do, and we'll tell you which applies.
