Overview

Most installations take a few hours to a day. When walls are already finished, we fish wiring through from the attic or basement and keep patching to a minimum. If you're remodeling, bring us in before the drywall goes up.

Every installation comes with a written estimate first, and we test everything before we leave. If your panel is full, see our page on subpanel installation.

Doorbells, Smart Home and Low-Voltage Wiring

Smaller jobs we do every week: wired and video doorbells, including new transformers for Ring and Nest and chime repair; wiring for smart switches, thermostats and cameras, adding neutral wires where devices need them; and network (Cat6), speaker and TV wiring, run neatly and kept clear of power wiring.

Questions

Good to Know

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Can you add outlets without opening my walls?

Usually. We fish new cable from the basement or attic and cut only a small opening for the new box.

Do small installations need a permit?

Replacing a device like-for-like usually doesn't. New circuits and most new wiring do, and we'll tell you which applies.
