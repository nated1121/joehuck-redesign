Barn Wiring in Yardley, PA

Barns are hard on electrical systems. Dust, moisture, temperature swings, animals and rodents all shorten the life of ordinary wiring. We wire and rewire barns and pole buildings on properties around Yardley and upper Bucks County with materials made for those conditions.

Signs a Barn Needs Electrical Work

  • Old wiring with chewed, cracked or exposed insulation
  • Extension cords run to stalls, fans or water heaters
  • Fixtures without covers, full of dust and cobwebs
  • Not enough light in stalls, aisles or the tack room
  • A new barn or pole building that needs power

Built for Barn Conditions

We use wiring methods suited to damp and dusty agricultural buildings, often PVC conduit with sealed boxes that keep out dust, moisture and rodents. Fixtures are enclosed and rated for wet or damp locations, and outlets are GFCI-protected. Wiring is kept out of reach of horses and livestock and protected where it could be damaged.

Power to the Barn

Barns are usually some distance from the house. We run an underground feeder sized for the load, install a subpanel with the barn's own grounding and a disconnect, and lay out circuits for lighting, outlets, fans, heated waterers, water heaters and any workshop equipment. The approach is similar to shed and garage wiring, with more attention to durability.

Lighting and Safety

Good light in aisles, stalls, wash racks and the tack room makes chores safer. We install bright, efficient LED fixtures with protective enclosures, and security and flood lighting outside with dusk-to-dawn or motion control.

What Affects the Cost

Barn size, distance from the house, the number of stalls and circuits, and whether old wiring has to be removed shape the price. We'll walk the building with you and quote in writing.

Fire Safety in Barns

Barns hold hay, bedding and dust, all of which burn easily, so electrical faults in barns can be especially dangerous. That's why we avoid exposed cable where animals or equipment can reach it, use enclosed fixtures that keep dust off hot bulbs, and protect outlets with GFCIs. Extension cords to heaters and water tank de-icers are a common fire cause; we replace them with properly protected circuits. Adding surge protection at the barn panel also helps protect fans, waterers and equipment from storm surges.

Planning a New Barn or Pole Building

If you're building, bring us in early. We'll plan outlets, lighting and circuits for stalls, the wash rack, the tack room and any workshop, and coordinate the feeder with your builder.
