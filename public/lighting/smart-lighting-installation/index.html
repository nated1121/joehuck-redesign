What Smart Lighting Can Do for You

  • Turn lights on at sunset and off at bedtime automatically
  • Set scenes for dinner, movies or morning with one button
  • Check and control lights from your phone when you're away
  • Voice control through Alexa, Google or Apple Home
  • Replace a wall of switches with a cleaner keypad

Smart Switches vs. Smart Bulbs

Smart switches and dimmers control whatever fixtures are on the circuit, work with any bulb and keep the wall switch working for guests. Smart bulbs are great for lamps and color effects, but they stop working as "smart" when someone flips the wall switch off. For most homes, we recommend smart switches and dimmers for fixed lighting.

The Wiring Smart Switches Need

Most smart switches need a neutral wire in the switch box. Many homes built before the 1980s don't have one there. We can add a neutral, choose a system designed to work without one, or use a wireless solution. Smart dimmers also need to be compatible with the LED fixtures they control, just like any dimmer. Our smart home wiring page covers thermostats, doorbells and other devices.

Planning a System

We'll help you pick a system that suits your home, whether that's Lutron Caseta or a similar platform, and set up rooms, scenes and schedules so the system is easy to live with. Motion sensor lighting pairs well with smart lighting in closets, laundry rooms and garages.

What Affects the Cost

The number of switches, whether neutrals need adding, multi-location control and the system you choose drive the price. Many homes start with a few rooms and expand later.

Starting Small

You don't have to automate the whole house at once. Many homeowners start with the outdoor lights on a sunset schedule, a smart dimmer in the kitchen and living room, and a scene for movie night. Once the system is set up, adding rooms later is simple, and every switch works together in the same app. We'll make sure the first pieces fit the system you'll grow into.

Questions

Good to Know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Do smart switches need a neutral wire?

Most do. We'll check your boxes and add a neutral or recommend a system that doesn't need one.

Will smart switches work if the internet goes down?

The switches still work as switches. App and voice control need the network.

Which smart lighting system should I use?

It depends on your home and how you'll use it. We'll recommend a reliable system that fits.

Can smart lighting work with my existing fixtures?

Yes. Smart switches and dimmers control your existing fixtures, as long as the fixtures and bulbs are compatible with the dimmer.
