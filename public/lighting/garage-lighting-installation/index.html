Signs Your Garage Needs Better Lighting

  • One bare bulb or a pull-chain fixture
  • Flickering or dead fluorescent shop lights
  • Shadows over the workbench and car
  • No switch at the house door
  • You want a gym, workshop or hobby space in the garage

Bright, Even Light

Modern LED linear fixtures and shop lights put out a lot of light for little power and work in cold weather, unlike fluorescent tubes that struggle in winter. We lay out fixtures to light the whole garage evenly, with extra over the workbench, and hardwire them on switches at the house door and the side door. See LED lighting retrofits if you'd like to convert existing fluorescent fixtures instead.

Motion Control

Walking into a garage with your arms full is the best argument for motion sensor lighting. We can put the lights on a sensor switch so they come on as you walk in.

Outlets and Circuits

Better lighting often goes with more outlets and circuits for tools, a freezer, a second fridge or a workbench, all GFCI-protected as garages require. For detached garages, workshops and car charging, see shed and garage wiring.

What Affects the Cost

The number of fixtures, ceiling height, switch locations and whether we're adding circuits drive the price. Most garage lighting jobs are done in a day.

Garages Used as Workshops and Gyms

If you use your garage as a workshop, gym or hobby space, plan the lighting for that use. Workbenches benefit from bright task lighting mounted directly above, so you don't work in your own shadow. Home gyms feel better with evenly distributed, bright light and no glare in mirrors. A 4000K to 5000K color temperature is usually best for detail work. We can also add ceiling outlets for garage door openers, retractable cord reels and extra lighting.

Garage Door Openers

Many garage door openers need a ceiling outlet. We'll add one if yours plugs into an extension cord, and make sure it's on an appropriate circuit.

Detached Garages

For detached garages, see our page on powering outbuildings. We'll handle the feeder, subpanel and lighting in one job.

Good to Know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Do LED lights work in a cold garage?

Yes. LEDs start instantly and stay bright in cold weather, unlike many fluorescent fixtures.

How many lights does a two-car garage need?

It depends on ceiling height and fixture type. Many two-car garages are well lit with four to six LED linear fixtures.

Can you add outlets while you're there?

Yes. Garage outlets need GFCI protection, and we can add circuits for tools and freezers.
