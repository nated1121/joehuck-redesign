Signs It's Time to Go LED

  • Fluorescent tubes flicker, hum or keep burning out
  • Ballasts keep failing, especially in garages and basements
  • Old recessed cans are hot, yellowed or use large halogen bulbs
  • High electric bills from lights that run all day
  • A business wants brighter, better-looking light at lower cost

Retrofitting Old Fixtures

Many fixtures can be converted instead of replaced. Old recessed cans take LED retrofit trims that screw into the socket and clip into the can, giving you a clean, modern look without cutting the ceiling. Fluorescent fixtures can take LED tubes, often with the ballast bypassed so there's nothing left to fail. We'll tell you whether a retrofit is the right call or whether the fixture is too worn and should be replaced.

Replacing With LED Fixtures

When fixtures are old, yellowed or damaged, new integrated LED fixtures are often the better choice. We install LED flat panels, linear fixtures, recessed lights and wraps in homes, garages and commercial spaces. For new recessed lighting, see recessed lighting installation. For garages and workshops, see garage lighting installation.

For Small Businesses

Offices, shops, restaurants and warehouses benefit the most, because their lights run long hours. We plan conversions to minimize disruption, working after hours or in phases. Better color quality also makes merchandise and workspaces look better.

Flicker and Dimming

LED conversions sometimes reveal old dimmers that aren't compatible, or fixtures with failing parts. We match dimmers to the new lights and fix any flicker. If a fixture is already failing, see lighting repair.

What Affects the Cost

The number of fixtures, retrofit versus replacement, ceiling height and working hours drive the price.

Choosing Color and Brightness

LEDs come in a range of color temperatures. For homes, warm white around 2700K to 3000K looks natural. Garages, basements and workspaces often benefit from 4000K. For retail, high color-rendering LEDs make products look their best. We'll recommend options for each space.

Questions

Good to Know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Can I put LED tubes in my fluorescent fixture?

Often yes. Some LED tubes work with the ballast; ballast-bypass tubes remove it entirely. We'll recommend the right type.

Are LED retrofit trims as good as new recessed lights?

For most rooms, yes. They give a clean, modern look without cutting the ceiling.

How much can a business save with LED?

It depends on hours and fixtures, but LED typically uses a fraction of the energy of fluorescent or halogen lighting.

Will LED lights work with my existing dimmers?

Sometimes. Many older dimmers were made for incandescent bulbs and cause LEDs to flicker. We check each dimmer and replace the ones that aren't compatible.
