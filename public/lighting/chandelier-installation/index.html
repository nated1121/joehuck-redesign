When You Need Help With a Chandelier

  • The fixture is heavy, or heavier than the one it's replacing
  • It's going in a two-story foyer or over a stairwell
  • It has dozens of crystals or parts to assemble
  • It needs to be centered over a table that isn't centered in the room
  • You want it on a dimmer

Proper Support

Standard ceiling boxes are rated for fixtures up to about 50 pounds. Many chandeliers weigh more, and they need a box and bracing rated for the weight, or support independent of the box, anchored to the framing. We check the weight and the ceiling structure first and add the support needed. A heavy fixture hanging from an ordinary box can pull loose over time.

Height and Position

Over a dining table, a chandelier usually hangs about 30 to 36 inches above the tabletop, and its width should suit the table. In a foyer, the bottom should clear the door and people walking under it, and in a two-story entry it often lines up with the second-floor window. When a table isn't centered under the existing box, we can relocate the box or add a swag hook.

High Ceilings

Two-story foyers and stairwells need the right equipment to work safely. We bring tall ladders or scaffolding as needed and protect your floors and stairs. We can also install a lift so the fixture can be lowered for cleaning.

Assembly and Dimming

We assemble the fixture, including crystals and shades, level it and put it on a dimmer matched to the bulbs. For kitchen islands and smaller spaces, see pendant lighting installation. For simpler fixtures, see ceiling light installation.

What Affects the Cost

Weight, ceiling height, assembly time and whether we need to add support or move the box drive the price.

Lowering an Existing Chandelier

Sometimes a chandelier is simply at the wrong height. We can shorten or lengthen the chain and wire, or move the canopy, so it hangs where it should. It's a small job that changes how a room feels.

Questions

Good to Know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

How high should a dining room chandelier hang?

Usually about 30 to 36 inches above the table, adjusted for ceiling height and the fixture's size.

Can my ceiling box hold a heavy chandelier?

Standard boxes are rated to about 50 pounds. Heavier fixtures need added support, which we install.

Can you install a chandelier in a two-story foyer?

Yes. We bring the equipment to work safely at height and protect your floors and stairs.

Can a chandelier go on a dimmer?

Yes, as long as the bulbs are dimmable. We match the dimmer to the bulbs so the fixture dims smoothly without flicker or buzzing.
