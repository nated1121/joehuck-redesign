Questions

Good to know.

Still have a question? Call 215-906-4634. You'll talk to an electrician, not a call center.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We look at the job, explain your options and give you a written price. You pay nothing unless you decide to go ahead.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. Many homes with a 200A service can add a Level 2 charger as is. Older 100A panels often need an upgrade or load management. We check your panel's capacity before quoting so you only pay for what you need.

Signs my panel needs replacing?

Breakers that trip often, a warm or buzzing panel, fuses, flickering lights when appliances start, or not enough slots for new circuits. Some older panel brands are also known safety risks. If any of this sounds familiar, get it looked at.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. When a job needs a permit, we pull it, meet the inspector and make sure the work passes before we call it done.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. Besides homes, we serve small businesses across Bucks County, from lighting retrofits and new circuits to tenant fit-outs and repairs.
