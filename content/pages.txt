## FAQ
Q: Is the estimate really free?
A: Yes. We look at the job, explain your options and give you a written price. You pay nothing unless you decide to go ahead.
Q: Why hire an electrician instead of a handyman?
A: Handymen do a bit of everything. We only do electrical work, and we've done it for over 40 years. Every job is done by licensed, insured electricians, under permit when needed, and inspected, so it's safe and to code.
Q: What are the signs my panel needs replacing?
A: Breakers that trip often, a warm or buzzing panel, fuses, flickering lights when appliances start, or no room for new circuits. Some older panel brands are also known safety risks. See our page on [electrical panel upgrades](@electrical-panel-upgrade).
Q: Do you pull permits?
A: Yes. When a job needs a permit, we pull it, meet the inspector and make sure the work passes before we call it done.
Q: Do you work on commercial properties?
A: Yes. Besides homes, we serve small businesses across Bucks County, from lighting retrofits and new circuits to repairs.
